VOLT: design mockups

Each card plays Volt's move set in a loop: idle, a fan of electric orbs, a lightning strike on a marked spot (sparks on the floor first), then the warp: he vanishes and comes back on the other side. The big view is 5x, next to UltraGuy for size; the row underneath is each pose at game scale. Arcs, glow and sparks are effects the game would add on top of the sprite (the layer Anvil and Magma use). CURRENT is today's two poses.
