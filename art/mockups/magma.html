MAGMA: design mockups

Each card plays Magma's move set in a loop: idle, the flashing wind-up, the charge (trailing fire), a leap with flame pillars where he lands, then a volley of lobbed fireballs. The big view is 5x, next to UltraGuy for size; the row underneath is each pose at game scale. Glow, embers, drips and fire are effects the game adds on top of the sprite (the same layer Anvil now uses). CURRENT is today's three poses.
