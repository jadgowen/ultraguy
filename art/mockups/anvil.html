ANVIL: design mockups

Each card plays Anvil's real move set in a loop, the way the fight uses it: idle, the crouch tell, the dash, a jump with its landing slam (ground waves), then the cannon. The big view is 5x, next to UltraGuy for size; the row underneath is each pose at game scale. The glow, sparks and squash-and-stretch are effects the game would add on top of the sprite. CURRENT is today's four poses for comparison.
