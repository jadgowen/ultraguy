ULTRA ARENA (raycaster mockup)

A first-person deathmatch in the style of the old 90s shooters: a raycast arena at 320x180, scaled up. Bots are the eight bosses (their real sprites), the pickups are UltraGuy's weapons, and the first to 10 frags wins. Press T to flip between three arena themes.

W/S or UP/DOWN move   A/D strafe   LEFT/RIGHT or Q/E turn (or click to use the mouse)   SPACE/click fire   1-5 weapons   T theme   M map
